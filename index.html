I am a third-year undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am currently a research assistant with Prof. Arman Cohan at Yale University, working on research agents and forecasting emerging directions in science. Before that I worked with Prof. Chen Zhao at NYU Shanghai on computer-use agents and reasoning-intensive multimodal retrieval. I also worked with Prof. Hai Zhao at Shanghai Jiao Tong University on reasoning and efficiency in large reasoning models. Alongside this, I intern at Yalotein Biotech, where I have built two agent applications.

My current research interest is in LLM agents, which I approach from two sides. One is benchmarks that show why agents fail to complete tasks on their own, and where the real bottleneck lies. The other is methods that address those bottlenecks. Earlier I worked on multimodal reasoning and retrieval, and on how efficiency techniques such as quantization and KV-cache compression affect the reasoning ability of large reasoning models.
