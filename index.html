I am a third-year undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am currently a research assistant with Prof. Arman Cohan at Yale University, and I work with Prof. Chen Zhao at NYU Shanghai on computer-use agents and multimodal reasoning.

My research is on agents that carry out real digital work over long horizons. I build controlled, realistic environments and benchmarks that isolate where agents fail, and turn those findings into methods that remove the bottlenecks — for instance, image-based memory representations for long-horizon execution. I am applying for PhD programs starting Fall 2027.
