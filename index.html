I am a fourth-year undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am currently a research assistant with Prof. Arman Cohan at Yale University, working on agentic search and AI for scientific discovery. Before that I worked with Prof. Chen Zhao at NYU Shanghai on reasoning-intensive multimodal retrieval and computer-use agents. I also worked with Prof. Hai Zhao at Shanghai Jiao Tong University on the efficiency of large reasoning models. Alongside this, I intern at Yalotein Biotech, where I have built two agent applications.

My long-term research goal is to develop language agents that help people reliably complete complex real-world tasks end to end and ultimately support open-ended scientific discovery. My research spans three connected directions:

  • Information Retrieval and Agentic Search. I study how agents acquire relevant evidence and retain useful knowledge throughout an extended search.
    MRMR
  • Computer Use Agents. I study how agents translate decisions into reliable actions through software interaction, execution feedback, and verification.
    GUI vs. CLI · OpenComputer
  • AI for Scientific Discovery. I study how agents can identify promising research directions from scientific evidence, toward open-ended investigation and discovery.
    BackTrend

Publications

BackTrend: Evaluating Scientific Weak-Signal Prediction via Backward Reconstruction

Xiao Zhou*, Yilun Zhao*, Owen Jiang*, Tiansheng Hu, Cai Xu, Manasi Patwardhan, Arman Cohan

Findings of EMNLP 2026 arXiv· Code· Data

A retrospective benchmark of 25 mature AI topics and 66 expert-validated weak signals: given a topic and a temporal evidence constraint, a system must recover the early precursors behind it, both underrecognized research problems and emerging methods.

GUI vs. CLI: Execution Bottlenecks in Screen-Only and Skill-Mediated Computer-Use Agents

Preprint arXiv· Code· Data

Holds goals and states fixed across screen-only and skill-mediated agents, isolating execution bottlenecks that belong to the interface rather than the model.

On the Efficiency-Safety Dilemma in Large Reasoning Models

Yifei Yang, Zouying Cao, Xingrui Wang, Xiao Zhou, Yuexian Li, Dongjie Yang, Hai Zhao

EMNLP 2026 Main Conference arXiv· Code

Studies how quantization, pruning, and KV-cache compression affect the efficiency, reasoning ability, and jailbreak robustness of large reasoning models, distinguishing genuine safety alignment from capability-induced failure.
