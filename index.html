About

I am a third-year undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (GPA 91.6/100, rank 9/98). I work on LLM agents that complete real digital work — operating computers the way people do, over long horizons, without a human watching each step.

My research runs in two directions that feed each other. Measurement: building controlled, realistic environments and benchmarks that isolate where agents fail — for example, holding goals and states fixed across screen-only and skill-mediated execution, which separates bottlenecks that belong to the interface from those that belong to the model. Repair: turning those findings into methods, such as image-based memory representations that keep long-horizon interaction history compact and usable.

I am currently working with Prof. Arman Cohan at Yale University, in person for summer and fall 2026. I am applying for PhD programs starting Fall 2027.
